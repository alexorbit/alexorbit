# Executive Summary

I design and build software systems that people depend on.

For nearly three decades I've worked across startups, product companies and consulting engagements, designing systems from the earliest idea to production.

My work sits at the intersection of software architecture, platform engineering, backend development, cloud infrastructure, AI and distributed systems.

Throughout my career I've naturally gravitated toward projects where architecture mattered more than frameworks, and engineering judgment mattered more than implementation volume.

I enjoy turning ambiguous business problems into systems that are simple, maintainable and capable of evolving over time.

More recently, my engineering workflow has evolved alongside modern AI tools. Rather than treating AI as a replacement for engineering, I use it as a force multiplier while remaining fully responsible for architecture, technical decisions, code quality and production outcomes.
